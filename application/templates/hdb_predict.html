Median resale price (overview)

Median HDB resale prices have increased steadily from 2017 to 2024, with stronger growth observed in larger flat types and well-connected towns. This section provides a high-level view of overall market movement to help users contextualise their price estimates.

  • Rising medians across most towns over 2017–2024.
  • 4-room and 5-room flats show stronger price growth than 2- and 3-room units.
  • Central / mature estates remain structurally more expensive than outer-ring towns.
